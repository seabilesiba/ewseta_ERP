    Welcome to CHIETA

    The Chemical Industries Education & Training Sector Authority (CHIETA) is a statutory body established by the Skills Development Act 97 of 1998. Our purpose is to facilitate skills development in the chemical industries sector. We are committed to ensuring that skills needs are identified and addressed through several initiatives, providing skills development to the industry and our stakeholders.
